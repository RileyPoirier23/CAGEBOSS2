# Agents: start here

Two games share this repo: **CAGE BOSS** (shipped) and **CAGE BOSS II: THE BUYBACK** (in development, its own exe).

1. Read `SKILLS.md`: how the repo is laid out, the commands, the canon, the text and screen rules, and the house rules.
2. Read `SEQUEL_HANDOFF.md`: what the last assistant did, what's broken and the to-do list.
3. When you finish, add your entry to the top of the turn log in `SEQUEL_HANDOFF.md`.

Never change game 1's behaviour unless Riley asks. Gate sequel changes behind `isII()` or the sequel's boot hooks.
